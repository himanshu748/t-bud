# Brand: T-Bud

_Status: active_

## Product truth

T-Bud lets agents prepare a real merchant-backed trek quote while people retain control of itinerary approval and temporary seat holds. Payment collection is intentionally unavailable in the pilot.

## Palette

- Checkout Indigo is the single brand accent.
- Cool near-white surfaces and deep indigo-black text create the premium fintech base.
- Green is reserved for confirmed policy and availability states.
- Red and amber are reserved for errors and warnings.

## Typography

- Display and body: Space Grotesk, weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.
- Protocol labels and numeric evidence: IBM Plex Mono, weights 400, 500 and 600.
- Use tight display tracking only at large sizes. Body copy remains open and readable.
- Use tabular figures for prices, capacity and timestamps.

## Voice

Direct, controlled and transparent. State what the agent can do, where it stops and what the human must approve. Avoid generic AI claims, travel romance and fabricated proof.

## Landing direction

- Product truth: machine-speed preparation, human-controlled commitment.
- Audience: hackathon judges, merchants and travellers evaluating trust.
- Desired feeling: premium, operational and unmistakably human-gated.
- Primary action: start a live quote.
- Visual concept: an authorization trail from brief to hold.
- Primary archetype: editorial narrative.
- Secondary influence: technical instrument.
- Page silhouette: oversized editorial opening, full-width product proof, then alternating evidence bands.
- Signature moment: the handshake visibly stops at the human gate.
- Avoid: generic AI gradients, floating dashboard cards and repetitive three-column feature grids.
